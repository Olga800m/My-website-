Who is training for?

Sessions adapt to your goals, fitness level, experience and physical needs. Depending on your goal, training can include strength and core stability, mobility, functional fitness, balance, coordination, running technique, endurance, speed, power or athletic development. Suitable for beginners, recreational athletes, runners and athletes preparing for competition.

What can a session include?

The session plan depends on your goal, current level and how you feel that day. It may include a short check-in, dynamic warm-up, running and coordination drills, strength and athletic exercises, core work, mobility and an easy finish. Not every session includes every element.

Competition preparation & athletic training

Goals may include 5K and 10K races, half marathons, HYROX (with a focus on running and athletic development) and track and field competitions. Depending on your goal, preparation may include training-load planning, running technique, strength, endurance, pace and race strategy. We agree on a specific plan after discussing your experience and goals.

Personal coaching, not a standard plan

As a two-time European Championships medalist, with an 800 m personal best of 1:58.64 and more than 15 years in elite sport, I bring practical experience to each session. We briefly discuss your goal, training background and current capacity before we begin.

Location and format

Training takes place outdoors in Köniz, for example at a stadium or in a park. The meeting point is agreed after booking. Home visits are not offered. For running technique and race preparation, see running coaching in the Bern region.
