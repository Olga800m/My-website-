Single session · 60 minutes · CHF 20 per person. Open group for 5–12 participants. If fewer than five people book, an alternative date will be offered.

What can a session include?

Depending on the focus, a session may combine a dynamic warm-up, running technique and coordination, functional strength, core work, mobility and a short endurance section. Exercises and intensity are adapted to different fitness levels; not every session includes every element.

Different fitness levels welcome

The training is suitable for beginners as well as active participants. As a former international 800 m runner, I focus on sound movement, practical strength, mobility and a clear training structure.

Confirmation and minimum group size

The open group runs with at least 5 and up to 12 participants. The session is confirmed no later than 12 hours before it starts. Use the link in your booking confirmation if you need to make changes.

Location

Group training takes place outdoors in Köniz. The exact meeting point is shared with the confirmed booking. Home visits are not offered.
