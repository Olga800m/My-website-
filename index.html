Athlete Profile

Olha
Lyakhova

Born 18 March 1992 in Rubizhne, Ukraine. Professional middle-distance runner specialising in the 800 metres, also competing in 400m, 1000m, 1500m, and 4×400m relay. Club: Avangard.

A medal-winning presence on the European stage since 2013, Olha has consistently represented Ukraine at the highest level — European Championships, World Athletics Championships, and prestigious international meetings.

She has been ranked No. 2 in Europe for 92 consecutive weeks and has competed against the world's best on every major stage.
